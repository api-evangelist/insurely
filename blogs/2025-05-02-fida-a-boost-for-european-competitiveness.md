The European Commission’s proposed Financial Data Access (FIDA) Regulation isn’t just another policy—it’s a game-changer for Europe’s financial sector. By promoting greater data accessibility and interoperability, FIDA aims to drive competition, fuel innovation, and empower consumers. Strengthening these foundations will not only make the financial sector more resilient but also enhance its global competitiveness.
